# Changelog

### Desktop refinements
 - Right-click menus stay inside the visible window, moving up or left near an edge; playlist submenus follow the repositioned menu
 - Queue and Lyrics share a resizable right panel that remembers its width; drag its left edge to resize, with song durations disappearing first as the queue narrows
 - Home and Search artwork scrolls cleanly to the content edge instead of cutting off behind a strip of padding
 - The search bar has equal padding above and below, with a divider separating it from scrolling content
 - Opening the library sidebar gives the main content a rounded top-left corner; collapsing it restores the straight top divider
 - Client-drawn window corners align with the outer outline, including the titlebar and immersive backdrop
